# Research Lab

**Scientific Exploration · Data Science · Artificial Intelligence**

Welcome to Research Lab, a workspace dedicated to exploring technical questions, studying scientific literature, conducting experiments, and documenting research findings.

The goal is to develop a rigorous approach to problem-solving through evidence, experimentation, and reproducible analysis.

## Research Areas

### 01. Artificial Intelligence
Exploring AI methods, machine learning techniques, intelligent systems, and their practical applications.

### 02. Data Science and Data Reliability
Investigating data quality, missing values, bias, preprocessing, reproducibility, and the reliability of data-driven insights.

### 03. Scientific Literature
Reviewing research papers, summarizing key findings, comparing methodologies, and identifying opportunities for further investigation.

### 04. Experimental Studies
Designing experiments, testing hypotheses, evaluating results, and documenting limitations.

### 05. Research Notes and Ideas
Recording research questions, preliminary observations, technical ideas, and potential directions for future studies.

## Research Approach

Research activities in this repository aim to follow these principles:

- **Evidence-based reasoning:** Supporting conclusions with appropriate evidence.
- **Clear methodology:** Documenting methods, assumptions, and analytical decisions.
- **Reproducibility:** Making experiments understandable and repeatable whenever possible.
- **Critical evaluation:** Examining limitations, uncertainty, and potential sources of bias.
- **Responsible research:** Respecting data licenses, privacy, attribution, and publication requirements.

## Documentation Standards

Where applicable, each research project should document:

1. Research question and objectives.
2. Background and related literature.
3. Data sources and methodology.
4. Experimental setup and analysis.
5. Results and interpretation.
6. Limitations and potential improvements.
7. References and reproducibility instructions.

## Current and Future Work

This repository will evolve as research questions are developed, literature is reviewed, and experiments are conducted.

Individual research projects may receive dedicated repositories when their scope, code, datasets, or documentation justify independent development.
