# Research Proposal

## 1. Title

Preparing Students for the AI-Driven Labor Market: Future Careers, Essential Skills and Learning Pathways Across Academic Fields.

## 2. Background

Artificial intelligence is changing how organizations perform tasks, organize work, and identify the skills required in different occupations.

University students need to understand these changes to make informed decisions about their education and professional development.

## 3. Research Problem

Students from different academic fields may face uncertainty about how AI will affect their future careers and which skills they should develop.

## 4. Research Objectives

- Examine the effects of AI on selected occupations.
- Identify relevant technical and human skills.
- Explore learning pathways discussed in existing research.
- Compare findings across selected academic fields.

## 5. Research Questions

1. How is AI changing the tasks and skills associated with selected occupations?
2. Which technical and human skills are emphasized in existing research?
3. What learning pathways are recommended to prepare students for these changes?
4. How do these requirements differ across academic fields?

## 6. Scope

The research will focus on publicly available literature, reports, and datasets.

## 7. Expected Contribution

The study aims to synthesize existing evidence and develop a structured understanding of career preparation in an AI-driven labor market.

## 8. Limitations

Findings will depend on the availability, quality, geographic coverage, and relevance of the selected sources.
