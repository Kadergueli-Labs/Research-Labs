# Preparing Students for the AI-Driven Labor Market

**Research Area:** Artificial Intelligence, Education, and Future of Work

## Overview

This research explores how artificial intelligence is transforming the labor market and what university students need to prepare for future careers.

It focuses on emerging career opportunities, essential skills, and learning pathways across different academic fields.

## Research Question

How can university students across different academic fields prepare for the skills and career requirements of an AI-driven labor market?

## Objectives

- Explore how AI is transforming occupations and professional skills.
- Identify technical and human skills that may become increasingly important.
- Examine learning pathways that can help students prepare for future careers.
- Compare relevant findings across different academic fields.

## Scope

The research considers university students from fields such as:

- Computer Science
- Business and Management
- Engineering
- Healthcare
- Law
- Communication
- Finance

## Research Approach

The study will use publicly available information and existing research.

Potential sources include scientific publications, institutional reports, and publicly accessible labor-market data.

No primary data collection is planned at this stage.

## Research Workflow

1. Define the research problem and objectives.
2. Review relevant scientific literature.
3. Identify suitable public data sources.
4. Develop the research methodology.
5. Analyze the available evidence.
6. Interpret the findings and limitations.
7. Prepare the final research report.
